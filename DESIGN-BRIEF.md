# Little Parandeh — Design Brief

- **Concept:** Scandinavian-inspired children’s boutique; calm, playful, tidy and practical.
- **Brand feeling:** safe, soft, joyful and polished without looking cartoonish.
- **Palette:** Milk White `#FFFDF8`, Sky `#BFDDF0`, Butter `#F6DFA0`, Peach `#F7CDB8`, Soft Navy `#2C3E5B`.
- **Typography:** Vazirmatn for Persian UI, DM Sans for Latin microcopy; system fallbacks included.
- **Layout:** spacious editorial grid, rounded image frames, calm contrast and soft asymmetry.
- **Signature 01:** gently animated organic blobs around the hero image.
- **Signature 02:** large rounded age filters for Baby, 1–3 and 4–8 years.
- **Signature 03:** product cards with soft image swap/zoom and size selection.
- **Interaction principle:** slow easing, no flashing effects, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
- **Technical intent:** static multi-page HTML, scoped CSS conventions, vanilla JavaScript and browser-local demo cart.
